# 🐙 Octopus is starting

Octopus opens in a new browser tab by itself. If your browser blocked the pop-up, open the **Ports** tab at the bottom of this window and click the 🌐 globe next to **Octopus (8000)**.

- **First time:** create your owner account. You don't need an invite code.
- **Other people:** they need your invite code. It's in the file `/workspaces/octopus-data/invite-code`, and Octopus prints it in the terminal when it starts. To let them in, right-click **Octopus (8000)** in the Ports tab → **Port Visibility → Public**, and send them the address.
- **Next time:** go to [github.com/codespaces](https://github.com/codespaces) and click this codespace. It starts again in under a minute, with your chats and models still there.
- It stops by itself after 30 minutes without typing in this window, which saves your free hours (GitHub gives 60 hours a month on this machine for free). Chatting in Octopus may not count as activity, so set a longer timeout (up to 4 hours) at [github.com/settings/codespaces](https://github.com/settings/codespaces) under **Default idle timeout**.
